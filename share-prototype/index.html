The growth hook

A living page can't be shared as a grey link.

The page itself moves — mood, motion, type that breathes. So the share preview is the trailer, and it moves too. A still image is only the fallback; everywhere it can, the page arrives alive. Below: the same page, dressed for five surfaces, for the writer who made it and the reader who just felt it.

The render

The preview isn't a card about the page. It is the page, miniature.

One engine, not three hand-made cards. It reads any page — its masthead, its lines and their types, its mood — and draws the render, then wraps that same render for every surface. Pick ＋ Compose in the picker and type your own to watch it happen live.

Two moments, two sheets

The writer is proud. The reader is moved.

Same render, different framing. The share sheet opens at two different moments and speaks in two different voices.

A · The writer · just published

"I made this."

The sheet slides up the instant a page goes live, headed "Your page is live." The render carries their handle; the default caption is authorship, not a hard sell — and one tap invites their first reader.

B · A reader · just finished

"You have to see this."

The sheet appears where the page ends, by "Keep going." The reader can tap a reaction — it becomes a hand-lettered stamp on the render, so the share carries a feeling, not just a URL.

Why it compounds

The reaction stamp puts a face on the hook.

Living Page already asks readers "same feeling?" at the end of every page. Share extends that: the feeling rides along, and the loop closes.

01 · Feel

Tap a reaction

At the end of the page, a reader picks a feeling — "same," "oof," "sending this." One tap, no typing.

02 · Stamp

It lands on the render

The reaction becomes a hand-lettered stamp. Now the shared, moving image says a person felt something here.

03 · Pull

Someone opens it

A moving render plus a real reaction is a promise. They tap, read, reach their own share moment. The hook re-arms.

Open questions · next

What to figure out to ship it.

Motion export

Format and file size for IG Story and X — video vs animated card — plus the still-frame fallback for surfaces that won't animate.

Render pipeline

Server-side headless render of the actual page engine into an OG image / loop, and cache invalidation when a page is edited.

Crop logic

Portrait page → landscape unfurl: ruled header + emphasis line + wordmark. Confirm which line is the "punch" per page.

Reaction taxonomy

A fixed set, or per-story suggested feelings that extend the end-of-page prompt.
