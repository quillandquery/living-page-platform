The departures board kept changing and I couldn't make it stop. Rome. Gone. Istanbul. Gone. My flight wasn't up yet. I was sweating. People rushed past me dragging suitcases, phones against their ears, shouting in languages I didn't know. I checked my passport again. Again. The stamp from last summer was still there, smudged. Suddenly they called my gate and I ran. I ran through the whole terminal, my bag slamming against my hip, and I made it just as the doors were closing. My heart would not slow down for an hour.
