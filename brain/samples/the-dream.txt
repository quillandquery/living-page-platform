In the dream the staircase went up into nothing and I kept climbing anyway. The walls were made of water and I could see my own face in them, doubled, tripled, wrong. A clock on the wall was melting, slow as honey. Somewhere a cassette tape had come unspooled and its ribbon floated in the air like a brown river. I was weightless. I reached for the red thread that hung from the ceiling and when I touched it everything shattered into bright pieces and I woke up.
